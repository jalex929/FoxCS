What to Search For

Your results are organized into four themes. Each one is a real angle to cover on your Vision Board, not just a search list.

Visual Style

What it looks like: colors, art, imagery.

    Interaction & Feel

    How it behaves and responds when you use it.

      Mood & Tone

      The emotional quality it gives off.

        Functionality

        How it's organized and what it actually does well.

          For your presentation: these four themes, Visual Style, Interaction & Feel, Mood & Tone, and Functionality, are a good way to organize what you say about each example on your Vision Board. You don't have to cover all four for every slide, but touching on more than one makes your reasoning a lot stronger than just "I like it."
          Next Step

          Head to the Vision Board assignment next. Start collecting real examples, screenshots, links, or clear descriptions, that show these qualities. Aim for at least 4–6 examples. If something outside your main category catches your eye too, include it, following genuine interest matters more than staying inside one lane.

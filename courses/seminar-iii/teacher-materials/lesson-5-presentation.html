Session A

Ratios & Rates

What two quantities am I comparing? Writing, simplifying, and scaling a comparison, then comparing rates

What Is a Ratio?

A ratio compares two quantities using division.

A classroom has 12 students and 3 computers. The ratio of students to computers is 12 to 3, or simplified, 4 to 1.
Order matters: the ratio of students to computers is different from the ratio of computers to students.

Part-to-Part or Part-to-Whole?

What is this ratio actually comparing?

A team has 5 forwards and 7 defenders (12 players total).
Part-to-part (forwards to defenders): 5:7
Part-to-whole (forwards to the team): 5:12
Students should identify which comparison a question is asking for before writing a ratio.

Writing a Ratio Three Ways

Same comparison, three equivalent forms.

3 to 23:23/2
The fraction form is a comparison, not automatically a request to divide to a decimal.

Simplifying Ratios

Divide both quantities by their greatest common factor.

18:12  →  18 ÷ 6 = 3, 12 ÷ 6 = 2  →  3:2
Simplifying does not change what is being compared. It only makes the comparison easier to work with.

Equivalent Ratios & Scaling

Multiply or divide both quantities by the same number.

Recipe ratio: 2 cups flour : 1 cup sugar. To scale up to 3 cups sugar, multiply both by 3: 6 cups flour : 3 cups sugar.
Both quantities must be scaled by the same factor to preserve the comparison.

Common Misconceptions

Does 3:2 mean the same thing as 2:3? Does simplifying change the comparison?

No, and no.

Order communicates which quantity is compared to which. 18:12 and 3:2 describe the same relationship at different scales.

Common Misconception

Is a part-to-part ratio the same as a fraction of the whole?

5 forwards : 7 defenders is not the same as saying forwards make up 5/7 of the team. The whole team has 12 players, so forwards make up 5/12 of the team.

What Is a Rate?

A rate compares two quantities measured in different units.

$18 per hour65 miles per hour$0.20 per ounce
Unlike many ratios, a rate must always keep its units attached. Every rate is a ratio, but not every ratio is a rate.

Unit Rate

How much for just one?

A movie theater sells 3 tickets for $24. 24 ÷ 3 = 8, so the unit rate is $8 per ticket.
A unit rate is a rate in which the second quantity is exactly one.

Comparing Rates: Best Buy

Calculate each unit rate, then compare directly.

Store A: 5 lb apples for $6.25  →  $1.25/lb
Store B: 8 lb apples for $9.60  →  $1.20/lb
Store B is the better value, even though its total price is higher.
Be careful whether a lower unit rate is better (price per ounce) or a higher unit rate is better (points per game).

Exit Ticket · Session A

  1. Write a ratio from a description, and say whether it is part-to-part or part-to-whole
  2. Generate an equivalent ratio
  3. Calculate a unit rate and use it to pick the better value between two options

Session B

Proportions & Unit Conversion

Setting up and solving a proportion, then applying the same reasoning to convert units and scale drawings

What Is a Proportion?

A statement that two ratios or rates are equal.

3/4 = 6/8. Both ratios represent the same comparison, 3 to 4, scaled differently.
A proportion can be used to find an unknown quantity when two ratios are known to be equal.

Setting Up a Proportion

Matching quantities go in matching positions.

A recipe uses 2 cups flour for every 3 cups sugar. How many cups of flour for 12 cups of sugar?
2/3 = x/12  (flour on top both sides, sugar on bottom both sides)
If the setup does not match position for position, the answer will be wrong even if the arithmetic is done correctly.

Solving a Proportion

Cross multiply, or find the unit rate and scale.

2/3 = x/12  →  2 × 12 = 3 × x  →  24 = 3x  →  x = 8
Unit rate method: 2 ÷ 3 ≈ 0.67 cups flour per cup sugar, then 0.67 × 12 = 8. Both methods should agree.

Checking Your Work

Substitute the answer back into the original comparison.

2/3 = 8/12?  8 ÷ 4 = 2, 12 ÷ 4 = 3  →  8/12 simplifies to 2/3. The solution checks.

Common Misconception

Can any two fractions be cross multiplied?

Cross multiplication only produces a correct answer when the proportion was set up with matching quantities in matching positions.

Unit Conversion

A conversion factor is a ratio equal to 1.

Convert 5 feet to inches: 5 feet × (12 inches / 1 foot) = 60 inches
The word "feet" appears once in the numerator position and once in the denominator position, and cancels.

Multi-Step Unit Conversion

Some conversions need more than one factor.

Convert 3 hours to seconds:
3 hours × (60 minutes / 1 hour) = 180 minutes
180 minutes × (60 seconds / 1 minute) = 10,800 seconds

Scale

Scale is a specific application of proportion.

A map has a scale of 1 inch : 50 miles. Two cities are 3.5 inches apart on the map.
1/50 = 3.5/x  →  x = 175 miles

Does My Answer Make Sense?

Size · Direction · Units · Context

Is the size realistic?Is lower or higher better here?Right unit?Makes sense in context?
Example: a unit rate of $45 per apple should immediately seem unreasonable, likely a division set up in the wrong direction.

  2. Solve a proportion and check the solution
  3. Convert one quantity and explain why the conversion factor was arranged that way

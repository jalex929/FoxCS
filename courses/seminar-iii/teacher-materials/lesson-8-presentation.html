The Real Shift This Week

Mixed problems don't announce which unit they belong to.

A recipe calls for 3 eggs for every 2 cups of flour. If a baker uses 9 eggs, how many cups of flour are needed?
This is the same recognition skill required on the ACT and in Lesson 5, now without the scaffolding of a single-topic week.

Day 4

Quarter 1 Growth Check

28 to 32 questions · 45 to 55 minutes · direct comparison to the Lesson 1 baseline

Before Students Begin

Remind Them

  • This measures growth across the whole quarter, not just this week.
  • Some questions will not announce which skill they require.
  • Attempting unfamiliar-looking questions still gives useful information.
  • Use the Lesson 1 routine throughout: STOP, FIND, CONNECT, TRY, CHECK.

If Time Remains

Initial Reaction

  • Which questions felt most familiar?
  • Which questions felt hardest to identify, not just hardest to solve?

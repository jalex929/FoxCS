The Quarter 1 Skill Map

Each skill built on the one before it. This week's Growth Check tests all six together — without telling you in advance which skill each question needs.

The Real Shift This Week

Which skill does this problem need — if it doesn't say?

A recipe calls for 3 eggs for every 2 cups of flour. A baker uses 9 eggs. How many cups of flour are needed?

This never announces "ratio problem." That recognition is the same skill the ACT and real situations demand.

Part 2

The Quarter 1 Growth Check

Same framing as the Week 2 baseline: informative, not punitive.

Why You're Taking This

  • It measures retention and transfer across all six Quarter 1 skills.
  • It compares directly to your Week 2 baseline where possible.
  • It surfaces what needs continued attention heading into Quarter 2.

Reading the Results

Not just a score.

Numbers and fractions can compare directly to your Week 2 baseline. Percent, ratios, expressions, and equations weren't tested then — those count as new mastery. You'll see the same Strength / Developing / Priority language from Week 2, not "you scored 21/31."

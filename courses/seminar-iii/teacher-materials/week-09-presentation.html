The Real Shift This Week

Which skill does this problem need — if it doesn't say?

A recipe calls for 3 eggs for every 2 cups of flour. A baker uses 9 eggs. How many cups of flour are needed?
This never announces "ratio problem." That recognition is the same skill the ACT and real situations demand.

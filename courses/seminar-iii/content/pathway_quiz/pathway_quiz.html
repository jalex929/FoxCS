Your Suggested Starting Pathway
Workforce Readiness

You're leaning toward moving directly into work, a trade, or a certificate program after graduation — not a 4-year college track. This pathway focuses on real job-readiness: resumes, applications, interviews, and workplace communication.

Your Suggested Starting Pathway
College Prep

You're leaning toward college after graduation, whether or not you know your exact major or school yet. This pathway focuses on getting you ready for admissions: applications, essays, and figuring out which schools actually fit you.

Your Suggested Starting Pathway
Dual Enrollment

You're leaning toward getting a real head start on college credit while you're still in high school. This is the most independent, self-paced option, and it's a great fit if you already know college is part of your plan.

Important: Pair This With College Prep

Dual Enrollment doesn't meet the whole period every day, so anyone choosing it also needs College Prep as a backup for the rest of that time. Think of your real pathway as "Dual Enrollment + College Prep," not Dual Enrollment on its own.

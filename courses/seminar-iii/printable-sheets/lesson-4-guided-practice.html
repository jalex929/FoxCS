Converting Fractions, Decimals, and Percents

Worked Example: Convert 3/4 to a percent
  1. Convert the fraction to a decimal: 3 ÷ 4 = 0.75.
  2. Convert the decimal to a percent: multiply by 100.
  3. 0.75 × 100 = 75, so 3/4 = 75%.

Now try these:

  1. Convert 0.6 to a percent.
  2. Convert 45% to a decimal.
  3. Convert 20% to a fraction in simplest form.
  4. Convert 4/5 to a percent.

Find the Part

Worked Example: What is 30% of 80?
  1. Convert the percent to a decimal: 30% = 0.30.
  2. Multiply by the whole: 0.30 × 80.
  3. 0.30 × 80 = 24, so 30% of 80 is 24.

Now try these:

  1. What is 20% of 45?
  2. What is 15% of 60?
  3. What is 75% of 32?

Find the Percent

Worked Example: 18 out of 24 learners completed an assignment. What percent completed it?
  1. Identify the part (18) and the whole (24).
  2. Divide the part by the whole: 18 ÷ 24 = 0.75.
  3. Convert to a percent: 0.75 = 75%.

Now try these:

  1. 9 is what percent of 36?
  2. 15 out of 20 questions correct is what percent?
  3. 21 is what percent of 28?

Find the Whole

Worked Example: $15 is 25% of what amount?
  1. Convert the percent to a decimal: 25% = 0.25.
  2. Divide the part by the decimal percent: 15 ÷ 0.25.
  3. 15 ÷ 0.25 = 60, so the whole is $60.

Now try these:

  1. 12 is 20% of what number?
  2. 36 is 60% of what number?
  3. 9 is 45% of what number?

Discounts and Sale Price

Worked Example: A $70 jacket is 20% off. Find the discount and the sale price.
  1. Find the discount amount: 0.20 × 70 = $14.
  2. Subtract the discount from the original price.
  3. 70 − 14 = $56, so the sale price is $56.

Now try these:

  1. A $50 shirt is 30% off. Find the discount amount and the sale price.
  2. A $120 pair of shoes is 15% off. Find the discount amount and the sale price.

Sales Tax and Percent Change

Worked Example: A $40 purchase has 10% tax. Find the tax and the total.
  1. Find the tax amount: 0.10 × 40 = $4.
  2. Add the tax to the original price.
  3. 40 + 4 = $44, so the total is $44.

Now try these:

  1. A $60 purchase has 8% tax. Find the tax and the total.
  2. A value increases from 40 to 52. What is the percent increase?
  3. A price decreases from $90 to $72. What is the percent decrease?

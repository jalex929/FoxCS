Quick Reference: Quarter 1 Skills

Numbers & Operations (Lesson 2)

Does my answer make sense?

  • Order of operations: parentheses, exponents, multiply/divide left to right, add/subtract left to right.
  • Same signs multiply/divide to positive, different signs to negative.
  • Check: operation, size, sign, unit, context.

Fractions & Decimals (Lesson 3)

Which form should I use?

  • A fraction is also a division: 3/4 = 3 ÷ 4 = 0.75.
  • Common denominator to add/subtract, no common denominator needed to multiply.
  • Place value decides which decimal is greater, not digit count.

Percent (Lesson 4)

What is this a percent of?

  • Percent means "per 100." Connects directly to fractions and decimals.
  • Find the original value before finding a percent change.
  • Discount, tax, and tip all start from identifying the correct base amount.

Ratios, Rates & Proportions (Lesson 5)

What two quantities am I comparing?

  • A ratio compares two quantities. A rate compares quantities with different units.
  • A unit rate is the amount for exactly one unit, the most reliable way to compare options.
  • A proportion sets two ratios or rates equal; solve with cross multiplication or the unit rate.

Variables & Expressions (Lesson 6)

What does this symbol represent?

  • A variable can change. A constant does not.
  • Combine like terms. Distribute across every term inside a grouping symbol.
  • An expression represents a value; it does not have an equals sign to solve.

Equations (Lesson 7)

What value makes both sides equal?

  • An equation states two expressions are equal.
  • Undo addition or subtraction before multiplication or division, working backward.
  • Check your solution by substituting it back into the original equation.

Discounts, Tax, and Tips

  1. A $90 jacket is 25% off. Find the discount amount and the sale price.
  2. A $200 television is 15% off. Find the discount amount and the sale price.
  3. A $65 purchase has 6% sales tax. Find the tax amount and the total cost.
  4. A meal costs $48. Estimate a reasonable 20% tip.
  5. A meal costs $32. Estimate a reasonable 15% tip.

Percent Change and Reasonableness

  1. A value increases from 30 to 45. What is the percent increase?
  2. A price decreases from $150 to $105. What is the percent decrease?
  3. A learner says 20% of 90 equals 1,800. Is this reasonable? Explain.
  4. A worker's wage rises from $12 to $15 per hour. What is the percent increase?

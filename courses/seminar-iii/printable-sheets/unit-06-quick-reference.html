Quick Reference: Variables & Expressions

Variables, Constants, Coefficients

In 3x + 7: x is a variable (its value can change), 7 is a constant (fixed value), and 3 is a coefficient (multiplies the variable).

An expression has no equal sign (3x + 7). An equation has one (3x + 7 = 22). Expressions are evaluated; equations are solved.

Translating Words to Expressions

  • Addition: more than, increased by, sum, total
  • Subtraction: less than, decreased by, difference
  • Multiplication: times, product, of, twice
  • Division: divided by, quotient, per

"Five less than a number" is x − 5, not 5 − x. "Less than" and "subtracted from" reverse the order.

Substituting and Evaluating

Replace the variable with its value, then follow the order of operations.

2x² + 5, x = 3 → 23

Use parentheses around a substituted negative value: 4(−3) − 6 = −18. Replace every occurrence of every variable.

Like Terms

Same variable, same power. 4x and 9x are like terms. 4x and 4x² are not (different powers). 3x and 3y are not (different variables).

Combine by adding or subtracting coefficients: 5x + 3x = 8x. The variable part stays the same.

The Distributive Property

The outside factor multiplies every term inside the grouping symbol.

3(x + 4) = 3x + 12−2(x + 6) = −2x − 12

A negative outside factor changes the sign of every term inside, not just the first one.

Seminar III · Lesson 8

Independent Practice

Show your work where it applies. Some problems don't tell you which skill to use.

Numbers, Fractions & Decimals

  1. Evaluate: 8 − 3 × (−2)
  2. Which is greater, −18 or −7?
  3. Convert 5/8 to a decimal.
  4. Order from least to greatest: 0.6, 3/4, 0.45, 1/2
  5. Estimate: 49 × 21
  6. Subtract: 6.2 − 1.75

Percent, Ratios, Rates & Proportions

  1. Find 35% of 80.
  2. A car travels 240 miles on 8 gallons of gas. What is the unit rate in miles per gallon?
  3. Solve for x: 3/7 = x/28
  4. A jacket priced at $50 is discounted 20%. What is the sale price?
  5. Simplify the ratio 20:35.
  6. A recipe uses 2 cups of rice for every 5 cups of water. How many cups of rice for 15 cups of water?

Variables, Expressions & Equations

  1. Evaluate 3x + 7 when x = 5.
  2. Simplify: 5x + 3 − 2x + 8
  3. Simplify: 2(x + 4) + 3x
  4. Solve: 3x − 5 = 16
  5. Solve: 2(x + 3) = 16
  6. Write an expression for "five less than triple a number."

Mixed, Unlabeled Application

These problems don't say which skill they need. Figure that out first.

  1. A recipe calls for 3 eggs for every 2 cups of flour. If a baker uses 12 eggs, how many cups of flour are needed?
  2. A phone plan costs a $25 activation fee plus a flat monthly rate. After 4 months, a customer has paid $145 total. What is the monthly rate?
  3. A learner's reading speed improved from 120 words per minute to 150 words per minute. What is the percent increase?
  4. A store sells notebooks at 3 for $6.75 or 5 for $10.50. Which option is the better deal per notebook?

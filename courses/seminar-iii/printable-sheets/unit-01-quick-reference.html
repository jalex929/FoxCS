Quick Reference: Academic Problem-Solving

The Five Questions

  1. What information do I have?
  2. What am I trying to determine?
  3. What skill or information could help?
  4. What strategy can I try?
  5. Does my answer make sense?

Short Version

STOP FIND CONNECT TRY CHECK

Same routine, five words instead of five sentences.

Knowledge Error

You don't know or remember something you need. Try this: review the concept or a worked example.

Process Error

Right idea, but the steps went wrong or out of order. Try this: compare your work to a correct example.

Execution Error

Right idea and steps, a small mistake carrying it out. Try this: slow down and check against an estimate.

Comprehension Error

You misunderstood what the question was asking. Try this: reread it and identify the actual task.

Strategy Error

You understood it, but the approach was inefficient. Try this: ask, "is there an easier way?"

Remember

Errors can overlap. Pick whichever category best explains what got in your way. Different problems need different responses, not just more practice.

Quick Reference: Percent

Percent Means Out of 100

25% = 25/100 = 0.25 = 1/4

Percent to decimal: divide by 100.

Decimal to percent: multiply by 100.

Three Percent Questions

  • Find the part: percent × whole
  • Find the percent: part ÷ whole
  • Find the whole: part ÷ percent

Money

  • Discount: subtract from original
  • Sales tax: add to original
  • Tip estimate: find 10%, then adjust

Percent Change

Change ÷ original value = percent change

Always use the original value, not the new value, as the denominator.

Does My Answer Make Sense?

  • Part smaller than whole when percent is under 100%
  • Discount goes down, tax goes up
  • Percent amount or final total, which did I find?

Guided Practice

Work through the worked example in each block together first. Then try the remaining problems using the same steps.

Block 1: Numbers, Fractions & Decimals

Worked Example: Evaluate −6 + 4 × 3
  1. Multiplication happens before addition: 4 × 3 = 12.
  2. −6 + 12 = 6.

Now try these:

  1. Compare: which is greater, −9 or −14?
  2. Convert 3/8 to a decimal.
  3. Add: 4.5 + 0.75
  4. Evaluate: 12 − 3 × 2

Block 2: Percent, Ratios, Rates & Proportions

Worked Example: Find 20% of 65
  1. 20% means 20 per 100, or 0.20 as a decimal.
  2. 0.20 × 65 = 13.

Now try these:

  1. A worker earns $104 for 8 hours. What is the unit rate?
  2. Solve for x: 4/5 = x/30
  3. A shirt is discounted 30% from $40. What is the sale price?
  4. Simplify the ratio 12:18.

Block 3: Variables, Expressions & Equations

Worked Example: Evaluate 5x − 3 when x = 4
  1. Substitute 4 for x: 5(4) − 3.
  2. 5(4) = 20, then 20 − 3 = 17.

Now try these:

  1. Simplify: 3(x + 2) + 4x
  2. Solve: 2x + 7 = 19
  3. Write an expression for "twelve more than twice a number."
  4. Solve: x/4 = 9

Recognizing a Skill Without Being Told

Worked Example: A recipe calls for 3 eggs for every 2 cups of flour. If a baker uses 9 eggs, how many cups of flour are needed?
  1. This doesn't say "ratio problem." Notice it is comparing two quantities: eggs and flour.
  2. Set up the ratio 3 eggs to 2 cups, and scale it: 9 eggs is 3 times as many as 3 eggs, so the flour scales the same way.
  3. 2 × 3 = 6 cups of flour.

Now try these (no skill label given, figure out what each problem needs):

  1. A gym membership costs a $30 sign-up fee plus a monthly rate. After 6 months, a member has paid $150 in total. What is the monthly rate?
  2. A learner's test score improved from 64 points to 80 points. What is the percent increase?

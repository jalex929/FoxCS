Quick Reference: Numbers & Operations

Order of Operations

  1. Parentheses (or other grouping symbols)
  2. Exponents
  3. Multiplication and Division, left to right
  4. Addition and Subtraction, left to right

Multiplication does not automatically come before division, and addition does not automatically come before subtraction. Each pair has equal priority, worked left to right.

Positive and Negative Numbers

On a number line, numbers farther right are greater. Every positive number is greater than every negative number. A negative number with a bigger digit is smaller, not bigger: −12 < −5.

Absolute value is distance from zero. It is never negative. |−8| = 8.

Sign Rules: Multiply & Divide

Same signs → positive Different signs → negative

Applies to multiplication and division only, not addition or subtraction.

Which Operation Do I Need?

  • Addition: combined, total, gained, increased by
  • Subtraction: difference, decreased, remaining
  • Multiplication: equal groups, rate × quantity
  • Division: split equally, amount per group

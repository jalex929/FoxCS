2.0 - Getting Ready for Certification

What you'll take away:
  • I have real Certiport and GMetrix accounts set up and ready to use.
  • I know why these accounts matter for this course.
  • I've submitted my account info so my teacher has a record of it.

This year you're working toward a real industry credential: the IT Specialist – Python certification. Two accounts make that possible, and you're setting both of them up today, before certification prep work actually starts, so there's nothing to chase down later in the year.

What These Two Accounts Are

Certiport is the official testing platform where you'll actually sit the IT Specialist – Python exam at the end of the certification track. GMetrix is practice software aligned to that same exam, used for hands-on prep exercises along the way. You need both, and you're the one creating each account, for the first time, today.

Step 1: Create Your Accounts

  1. Go to the Certiport account creation page and create your account. Use your FoxCS codename or the identifying info your teacher tells you to use, not personal details you wouldn't otherwise share.
  2. Go to the GMetrix account creation page and create your account the same way.
Use the Same Username and Password for Both

Make your Certiport username/password and your GMetrix username/password identical. One username and password to remember instead of two. Pick something you'll actually remember, and write it down somewhere safe before you move on. This isn't the strongest possible security habit, but for this class it's the tradeoff worth making: a forgotten password blocks your certification prep, and that's worse than the small risk here.

Step 2: Write Down What You Used

Before you go any further, write your username and password down somewhere you won't lose it. You'll need both again all year.

Password: ______________________

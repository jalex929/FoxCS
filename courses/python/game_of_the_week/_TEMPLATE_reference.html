How to Play

You already know how to play this one. That's exactly why we're starting here — it's a lot easier to notice how a game works when you're not busy learning the rules for the first time.

What This Game Is Actually Doing

Why not knowing what's coming is the whole point

If you always knew what your opponent was about to throw, you'd never lose — but it would stop being a game. That's hidden information at work, one of the most common mechanics in game design. The tension you feel not knowing? That's the aesthetic it creates.

One more mechanic in play: rock beats scissors, scissors beats paper, paper beats rock — no single best choice. Designers call that balance. Take it away and the game falls apart fast.

Your Path This Year

Welcome. This course works a little differently than a typical class: instead of everyone following the exact same sequence all year, you'll get to choose the pathway that fits what you want to build. Later in this unit (Lesson 0.9), you'll learn about your two options and make that choice for yourself. For now, just know that choice is coming, and it's yours to make, not something decided for you by which class period you're sitting in.

Whichever pathway you end up choosing, this year is built around the same core ideas: understanding the person who's going to use what you build, breaking big problems into pieces you can actually solve, and getting comfortable with the fact that things won't work on the first try, and that's completely normal.

The next several lessons cover ground that applies no matter which pathway you pick: how learning actually works in this class, using your tools, troubleshooting when something breaks, computational thinking, problem-solving, getting unstuck, and academic integrity. Then, in Lesson 0.9, you'll learn about your two pathway options and choose.

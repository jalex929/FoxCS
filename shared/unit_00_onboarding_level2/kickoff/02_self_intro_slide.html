Kickoff Activity — Introduce Yourself

What you'll do:
  • I can build one slide that introduces myself, using my MakeCode avatar.

Now that your avatar is ready, let's use it. You're going to build one slide in Google Slides that introduces you to the rest of the class.

Teacher to-do, not shown to Learners once filled in

Decide whether this is one shared class deck (every Learner adds a slide to the same deck) or individual files, then replace this box with the real link or instructions. See 00-project-overview/kickoff-avatar-and-intro-activity.md's Part 2 open item.

What to Include

Your slide should have your avatar image on it, plus a few things about you:

Your name
Your grade
Something you're looking forward to in this class
Example: "I'm looking forward to finally making something I can actually play."
A hobby or two
Your favorite game
Any kind counts: console, mobile, tabletop, card game, whatever you actually enjoy playing.

How to Add Your Avatar to the Slide

  1. In Google Slides, click Insert, then Image, then Upload from computer.
  2. Find avatar.bmp in your Kickoff folder and select it.
  3. Resize and place it wherever looks good on your slide.

What To Do Next

  1. Build your slide with everything listed above.
  2. Save it. If it's a Google Slides file, it saves automatically as you work.
  3. Let your teacher know it's ready, or submit it the way you're told in class.
Google Slides saves automatically as you work.

Kickoff Activity — Introduce Yourself

What you'll do:
  • I can build one slide that introduces myself, using my MakeCode avatar.

Now that your avatar is ready, let's use it. You're going to build one slide in Google Slides that introduces you to the rest of the class.

  1. Go to slides.google.com and create a new, blank presentation. This is your own file, just for this activity.
  2. Give it a title, like your name and "Introduction."

What to Include

Your slide should have your avatar image on it, plus a few things about you:

Your name
Your grade
Something you're looking forward to in this class
Example: "I'm looking forward to finally making something I can actually play."
A hobby or two
Your favorite game
Any kind counts: console, mobile, tabletop, card game, whatever you actually enjoy playing.

How to Add Your Avatar to the Slide

  1. In Google Slides, click Insert, then Image, then Upload from computer.
  2. Find avatar.bmp in your Kickoff folder and select it.
  3. Resize and place it wherever looks good on your slide.

Last Step: Make Your Avatar Your Moodle Profile Picture

Your avatar isn't just for this slide, it's how you'll show up across the whole Moodle site. Set it as your profile picture:

  1. Click your name in the top right of any Moodle page, then Preferences.
  2. Under "User account," click Edit profile.
  3. Scroll down to the User picture section.
  4. Drag avatar.bmp into the upload box, or click it and browse to your Kickoff folder.
  5. Scroll to the bottom and click Update profile.
If your avatar looks blurry once it's uploaded

That almost always means it wasn't actually resized to 500 by 500 before you saved it. Go back to Make Your Avatar and redo Steps 5 through 7 (select the marquee tool, resize the canvas to 500 by 500, then stretch your drawing to fill it), then save and re-upload it here.

If you can't see or change the width/height number fields in Step 6, don't struggle with it alone, check in with Mr. Fox. He can help you create a new image at the right size and get it transferred over.

What To Do Next

  1. Build your slide with everything listed above.
  2. Save it. If it's a Google Slides file, it saves automatically as you work.
  3. Set your avatar as your Moodle profile picture (steps above).
  4. Let your teacher know it's ready, or submit it the way you're told in class.
Google Slides saves automatically as you work.

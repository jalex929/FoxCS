Course Onboarding

Welcome. Before your pathway's Unit 1 begins, this unit gets you set up: how the course works, your tools, how to solve problems and get unstuck, academic integrity, and choosing which pathway you'll follow. There's no unit project, your journal entries along the way are this unit's reflective work.

Lessons

  • 0.1 Welcome
    What this course is, and the pathway choice ahead of you.
  • 0.2 How Learning Works
    What mastery actually means, and the Reinforce/Core/Extend levels.
  • 0.3 Using Your Tools
    Setting up tools for both pathways, since you haven't chosen yet.
  • 0.4 Troubleshooting Is Learning
    A real process for when something breaks, instead of guessing.
  • 0.5 Introduction to Computational Thinking
    Decomposition, pattern recognition, abstraction, algorithmic thinking.
  • 0.6 How Problem-Solving Works
    A five-step process for tackling a new problem.
  • 0.7 Getting Unstuck
    What to try before asking for help, and how to ask well.
  • 0.8 Academic Integrity: Doing Your Own Work
    Why it matters, partner work, helping others the right way, AI use, and what happens if the line gets crossed.
  • 0.9 Choosing Your Pathway
    Web Dev or Game Design/Unity. Your choice, not tied to which class you're in.
  • Kickoff Activity: Make Your Avatar
    A short, fun MakeCode Arcade activity and a one-slide self-introduction.

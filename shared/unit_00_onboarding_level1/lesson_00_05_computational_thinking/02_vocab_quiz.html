0.5.2 — Vocab Quiz: Computational Thinking

Do the quiz in Moodle instead

This page is kept for reference, but your real Vocab Quiz is its own activity in this Unit 0 section on Moodle — look for "0.5.2 Vocab Quiz: Computational Thinking (Drag and Drop)" in your course. That version saves your progress automatically. The quiz below does not save here, so don't use its Save button.

Place each term onto the definition you think matches (drag, or click a term then click a definition) — it's fine to place all four before checking anything. Click Check Answers when you're ready to see what's right. Correct pairs lock in; wrong ones stay marked so you can see what you guessed, and you can click one to try a different term. Get all four right, then finish the reflection below, to unlock saving.

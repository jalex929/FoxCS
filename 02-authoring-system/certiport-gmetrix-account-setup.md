**Added 2026-08-31**, per Jay: every CS pathway (Game I, Web Dev, Software Dev, Game Design/Unity) needs students to set up their real Certiport and GMetrix accounts early, ideally in Unit 1 even before real lesson content starts, so accounts exist well before certification prep work begins and Jay isn't chasing missing accounts later in the year (he's going on leave in 2 weeks and wants routines in place first).

## Sequencing, per Jay

1. **Intro/orientation first** — Jay floated having students watch "intro to the course" videos as the very first Unit 1 step, just to confirm they have everything they need before diving in. **Not yet built or sourced** — no such video exists in this repo yet. Flagged as Jay's own follow-up, not fabricated here.
2. **Account setup** — students create their Certiport and GMetrix accounts. Jay's explicit ask: encourage students to use the **same username and password for both platforms**, since that's easiest to remember (not a security best practice in general, but a deliberate simplicity tradeoff Jay is making for a class of students who'll otherwise lose/forget credentials).
3. **Credential tracking form** — every student fills out a Google Form recording the username/password they actually used for each platform, submitted early so Jay has a real record before anyone's deep into certification prep. This mirrors the existing pattern of tracking initial passwords in the private roster Sheet (`06-data-and-spreadsheets/roster-schema.md`) — same category of sensitive-but-necessary teacher record-keeping, not a new kind of risk.

## Draft Google Form Questions

Jay to review/adjust and actually build in his own Drive (same pattern as the Seminar III survey — drafted here as questions, built by Jay). Suggested title: **"Certiport / GMetrix Account Setup — FoxCS"**

1. **Codename** (short answer) — "Enter your FoxCS codename exactly as it appears on Moodle (e.g. G1-NOVA)."
2. **Pathway** (multiple choice) — Game I (Python) / Web Dev / Software Dev / Game Design/Unity.
3. **Certiport username** (short answer)
4. **Certiport password** (short answer)

8. **Anything not working / need help with?** (paragraph, optional)

**Response destination:** a Sheet in the same private/restricted Drive location as the roster (`FoxCS Private Roster 2026-27` folder or a sibling sheet, not the public-facing repo) — this is real student credential data, same handling rules as the roster's `initial_password` column.
